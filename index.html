Origins & Essence

Calligraphy is not just writing; it is the arrangement of beautiful symbols by hand. It requires skills for positioning and inscribing words to exhibit integrity, harmony, ancestry, rhythm, and creative fire.

The word itself comes from the Greek kalli (beautiful) and graphia (writing). QuickCalligraphy is designed to bridge the gap between ancient tradition and modern application, providing tools for everyone from hobbyists to professional graphic designers.
